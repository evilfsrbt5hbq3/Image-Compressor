Image Compression FAQ

How do I compress an image online for free?

Drag and drop your image into the box above or click "Choose Images". Adjust the quality slider if needed, then click "Compress Images". Your compressed image is ready to download instantly - no sign-up required.

Is it safe to compress images online?

Yes. This tool processes all images directly in your browser using JavaScript. Your images are never uploaded to any server, so they remain 100% private and secure on your device.

How much can I reduce an image file size?

Most JPEG photos can be reduced by 50-80% with no visible quality loss. PNG files benefit even more. The exact reduction depends on the original image and the quality setting you choose.

What image formats are supported?

This tool supports JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF images. Compressed output is saved as JPG, PNG or WebP depending on your source file format.

Will image quality be reduced after compression?

Lowering the quality slider reduces file size and may slightly reduce sharpness on high zoom. For the best balance, use a quality of 70-80%, which looks nearly identical to the original to the human eye.
